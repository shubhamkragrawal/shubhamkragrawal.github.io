How I work

Reliability is a design choice.

I’m most useful where model behavior, data quality, and user decisions meet—and where the team cares about knowing what the evidence does and does not establish.

Measure the right layer

Keep model capability separate from system outcomes, and attach every headline number to its evaluation conditions.

Design for failure

Use typed boundaries, deterministic checks, provenance, safe fallbacks, and explicit refusal when uncertainty cannot be resolved.

Ship with context

Connect technical implementation to the operator, client, or stakeholder who must understand and act on the result.
