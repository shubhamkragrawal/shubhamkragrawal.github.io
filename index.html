Statistical NLP

Regulatory-pathway text analysis

Designed a three-arm study of public FDA reviews using TF-IDF, biomedical sentence embeddings, document-level inference, permutation tests, bootstrap analysis, and prespecified checks for therapeutic-area, reviewer, length, product-name, and reference-family confounds.

Analyzed 148 documents and 10,839 labeled sections; found within-corpus 351(k) pathway distinctiveness while the original greater-homogeneity hypotheses were not supported.

TF-IDFBiomedical embeddingsPermutation testsBootstrap

How I work

Reliability is a design choice.

I’m most useful where model behavior, data quality, and user decisions meet—and where the team cares about knowing what the evidence does and does not establish.

Measure the right layer

Keep model capability separate from system outcomes, and attach every headline number to its evaluation conditions.

Design for failure

Use typed boundaries, deterministic checks, provenance, safe fallbacks, and explicit refusal when uncertainty cannot be resolved.

Ship with context

Connect technical implementation to the operator, client, or stakeholder who must understand and act on the result.
